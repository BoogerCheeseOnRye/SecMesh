The thesis

Centralized proxy security is tuned by rules.
A field doesn't need tuning — it self-stabilizes.

Standard WAF/IDS policy is a static, human-ordered rule set: it does the same defensive work whether attack load is low or crushing, degrades in cliff-steps, and hands your traffic to a third party's network. SecMesh replaces the rule stack with a scalar health field. Defensive work is proportional to attack load — measured, not claimed: blocked ≈ 187 at threat 0.05 rises to ≈ 597 at threat 0.5, while intrusions stay at zero from the same unchanged config.

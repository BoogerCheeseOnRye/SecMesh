⚡ Quantum→Universe

Three slices of physics, from Planck foam to cosmic web

Quantum Foam

At 10⁻³⁵ meters, spacetime isn't smooth — it boils. Virtual particles pop in and out of existence. Entanglement links distant points instantly. Particles tunnel through barriers they classically couldn't cross. This is the substrate from which all forces emerge.

Scale: Planck → Quark Quantum ħ: 0.8 Tunneling: 0.5 Entanglement: 0.7 Decoherence: 0.01 Planck jitter: 0.5

Atomic Lattice

Electrons settle into discrete shells around nuclei. The fine-structure constant governs electromagnetic coupling. Strong force binds quarks into protons and neutrons. Higgs field gives mass. Crystal bonds form — each atom a node in a quantum graph, sharing electrons through tunneling.

Scale: Atomic → Molecular Energy shells: 7 Strong force: 0.4 Fine α: 1.0 Higgs field: 0.5 Gravity: 0.01

Galactic Web

Dark matter forms filaments stretching millions of light-years. Galaxies flow along these threads like dew on a spiderweb. Dark energy pushes voids apart, accelerating expansion. Gravity curves spacetime — light bends around clusters. The universe is a foam of walls and voids.

Scale: Galactic → Universal Gravity: 0.8 Dark energy: 0.3 Curvature: 0.7 Inflation: 0.1 Alignment: 0.01

Why This Matters for AI & Security

Graph Neural Networks

Nodes = neurons/atoms/galaxies. Edges = synapses/bonds/filaments. Message passing across the graph mirrors how our simulator propagates activation, entanglement, and gravity. Training = adjusting edge weights (alignment force) to minimize energy.

Transformer Attention

Entanglement = attention weights. Every token attends to every other — non-local correlation. Our entangle parameter tunes correlation decay with distance. Multi-head attention ≈ multiple entanglement channels.

Diffusion Models

Trail deposition + evaporation = forward/reverse diffusion. Agents deposit pheromone (score), diffusion blurs it (noise), chemotaxis follows gradient (denoising). Slime mold solves the same variational problem as DDPM sampling.

Adversarial Robustness

Tunneling = adversarial escape. Particles quantum-tunnel through energy barriers. In latent space, this is an adversarial example crossing a decision boundary. tunnel parameter controls barrier penetration rate — a direct knob for robustness testing.

Cryptographic Entropy

Planck-scale jitter (planckScale) injects true quantum randomness. Wavefunction collapse on measurement = one-time pad generation. Entangled pairs across nodes = device-independent QKD (Ekert protocol). Decoherence rate = key expiration.

Anomaly Detection

Dark energy repulsion (darkEnergy) pushes outliers to voids. Energy shells quantize normal behavior into discrete levels. Anything between shells or in a void = anomaly. Used for network intrusion, fraud, side-channel detection.

Federated Learning

Nodes = clients. Alignment force = model averaging. Repulsion = personalization (prevent collapse to global model). Curvature = non-IID data geometry. The simulator visualizes convergence dynamics across heterogeneous clients.

Zero-Trust Topology

Connections form only when trust threshold exceeded (connectionThreshold). Entanglement = shared secrets. Tunneling = covert channel risk. The graph structure is the policy — visualize, audit, and harden in real time.
